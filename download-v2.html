📋 Installation Steps

  • Download the APK file using the button above
  • Go to Settings → Security on your Android device
  • Enable "Install from Unknown Sources"
  • Open the downloaded APK file
  • Tap "Install" and wait for completion
  • Launch the app and login with your cafe credentials

📱 System Requirements

Android Version: 7.0 (Nougat) or higher
Storage Space: At least 200 MB free
Internet: Required for app functionality
Permissions: Camera (for QR scanning), Storage (for printing)

🔄 Upgrading from v1.x

  • Download the new v2.0.0 APK
  • Install over your existing app
  • Your data will be preserved automatically
  • Login with your existing credentials
  • Enjoy the new features!
