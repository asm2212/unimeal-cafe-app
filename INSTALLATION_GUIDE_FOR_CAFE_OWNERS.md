# UniMeal Cafe App - Installation Guide for Cafe Owners

## 📱 How to Install UniMeal Cafe App on Your Android Phone

### Requirements:
- Android phone (Android 7.0 or higher)
- Internet connection
- At least 200 MB free storage

---

## Step 1: Enable App Installation from Unknown Sources

Before installing the app, you need to allow your phone to install apps from sources other than Google Play Store.

### For Android 8.0 and above:
1. Open **Settings** on your phone
2. Go to **Apps & notifications** (or **Apps**)
3. Tap **Advanced** → **Special app access**
4. Tap **Install unknown apps**
5. Select your **browser** (Chrome, Firefox, etc.)
6. Enable **Allow from this source**

### For Android 7.0 - 7.1:
1. Open **Settings** on your phone
2. Go to **Security** (or **Lock screen and security**)
3. Enable **Unknown sources**
4. Tap **OK** when prompted

---

## Step 2: Download the App

### Option A: Using Download Link
1. Open the download link sent to you via WhatsApp/Telegram/Email
2. Tap the link to start downloading
3. Wait for download to complete (86 MB - may take 1-5 minutes)

### Option B: Using QR Code
1. Open your phone's camera
2. Point it at the QR code
3. Tap the notification that appears
4. Download will start automatically

---

## Step 3: Install the App

1. Once download is complete, tap **Open** in the notification
   - Or go to **Downloads** folder and tap the APK file
2. You'll see "Do you want to install this application?"
3. Tap **Install**
4. Wait for installation to complete (10-30 seconds)
5. Tap **Open** to launch the app

---

## Step 4: Grant Permissions

When you first open the app, it will ask for permissions:

1. **Camera** - To scan QR codes for student verification
2. **Storage** - To save QR codes and receipts
3. **Photos/Media** - To access media library

**Tap "Allow" for each permission** - these are necessary for the app to work properly.

---

## Step 5: Login to Your Account

1. On the login screen, enter:
   - **Username or Phone**: Your cafe owner username
   - **Password**: Your password (provided by admin)

2. Tap **Login**

3. You'll be taken to your cafe dashboard

---

## 🎉 You're Ready!

You can now:
- ✅ Register students
- ✅ Generate QR codes
- ✅ Scan student QR codes
- ✅ Process meal transactions
- ✅ Manage student balances
- ✅ View transaction history

## 🔄 Updating the App

When a new version is available:

1. You'll receive a notification or message
2. Download the new APK using the same link
3. Install it (it will replace the old version)
4. Your data will be preserved

---

## ⚠️ Important Security Notes

1. **Only download from official links** sent by UniMeal admin
2. **Never share your login credentials** with anyone
3. **Keep your app updated** for security and new features
4. **Logout when not in use** if others have access to your phone

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Problem: "App not installed" error
**Solution:**
1. Uninstall the old version first (if any)
2. Make sure you have enough storage space
3. Try downloading again

### Problem: "Parse error" when installing
**Solution:**
1. Delete the downloaded file
2. Download again (file may be corrupted)
3. Make sure your Android version is 7.0 or higher

### Problem: App crashes when opening
**Solution:**
1. Go to Settings → Apps → UniMeal Cafe
2. Tap "Storage" → "Clear Cache"
3. Restart your phone
4. Open the app again

### Problem: Can't login
**Solution:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Make sure you're using the correct username/password
3. Contact admin if you forgot your password

### Problem: Camera not working for QR scanning
**Solution:**
1. Go to Settings → Apps → UniMeal Cafe → Permissions
2. Make sure Camera permission is enabled
3. Restart the app
