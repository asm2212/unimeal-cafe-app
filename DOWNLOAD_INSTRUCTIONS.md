### Installation Steps

1. **Download the APK**
   - Click the download link above or visit the GitHub repository
   - The APK file is approximately 86 MB

2. **Enable Unknown Sources**
   - Go to your Android device's Settings
   - Navigate to Security or Privacy settings
   - Enable "Install from Unknown Sources" or "Allow from this source" for your browser

3. **Install the App**
   - Open the downloaded APK file
   - Tap "Install"
   - Wait for the installation to complete
   - Tap "Open" to launch the app

4. **Launch the App**
   - Look for the UniMeal Cafe icon with a coffee cup design
   - Tap to open and start using the app

## ☕ App Features

- **Cafe Management**: Manage your cafe operations
- **Student Registration**: Register and manage students
- **QR Code System**: Generate and scan QR codes for transactions
- **Balance Management**: Track and update student balances
- **Transaction History**: View detailed transaction records
- **Dashboard**: Real-time statistics and insights
